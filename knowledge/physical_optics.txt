TOPIC: Wave Optics & Scalar Diffraction
FORMALISM: Angular Spectrum Method (ASM) / FFT Propagation
- Use for: Propagating fields between parallel planes (free space, apertures).
- ALGORITHM:
  1. Take FFT of field `U(x,y)`.
  2. Multiply by Transfer Function `H(fx, fy) = exp(i * k * z * sqrt(1 - (lambda*fx)^2 - (lambda*fy)^2))`.
  3. Take Inverse FFT.
- SAMPLING CONSTRAINT: To avoid aliasing, `dx < lambda / (2 * NA)`. If propagating far distances, use Fraunhofer approximation instead.

FORMALISM: Fraunhofer Diffraction (Far Field)
- Formula: The far-field pattern is proportional to the Fourier Transform of the aperture function.
- Scale factor: `x_screen = fx * lambda * z`.

PITFALLS:
- Zero-padding is critical for FFTs to prevent "circular convolution" artifacts (edges of beam wrapping around).
- Ensure energy conservation (sum of `abs(U)**2`) remains constant during propagation.
