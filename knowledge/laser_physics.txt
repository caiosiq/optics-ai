TOPIC: Laser Cavity & Resonator Physics
FORMALISM: Round-Trip ABCD Matrix stability.
- CONDITION: Cavity is stable if `abs(trace(M_round_trip)) <= 2.0`.
- BEAM WAIST: Solve for complex q-parameter: `1/q = (D-A)/(2B) - i * sqrt(1 - ( (A+D)/2 )^2 ) / B`.
  - Waist `w0 = sqrt( -lambda / (pi * imag(1/q)) )`.

GAIN PHYSICS (If requested):
- Small Signal Gain: `G0 = exp(sigma * N * L)`.
- Threshold: `R1 * R2 * exp(2 * g_th * L) = 1`.
- Saturation Intensity: `Isat = h * freq / (sigma * tau)`.

THERMAL LENSING:
- Only significant for Pump Power > 1W (Solid State).
- Model as a thin lens with `f_th = (K * A) / (P_heat * dn/dT)`.
