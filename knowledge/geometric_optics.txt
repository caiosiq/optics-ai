TOPIC: Geometric Optics & Ray Tracing
FORMALISM 1: Paraxial (ABCD Matrices)
- Use for: Simple stability checks, first-order system design, Gaussian beams.
- MATRIX ORDER: Light travels Left-to-Right, but Matrices multiply Right-to-Left.
  - `M_system = M_last @ ... @ M_first`
- COMMON MATRICES:
  - Free Space (d): `[[1, d], [0, 1]]`
  - Thin Lens (f): `[[1, 0], [-1/f, 1]]`
  - Curved Interface (n1->n2, R): `[[1, 0], [(n1-n2)/(R*n2), n1/n2]]`

FORMALISM 2: Exact Vector Ray Tracing
- Use for: Aberrations, high-NA systems, non-paraxial rays.
- STATE: Ray is defined by Position `r = [x, y, z]` and Direction Cosine `k = [kx, ky, kz]`.
- SNELL'S LAW (Vector Form): `v_out = (n1/n2) * v_in + (gamma) * normal`, where gamma is derived from cross products. Do not use `theta1 = n2/n1 sin(theta2)` directly in 3D code.

PITFALLS:
- Don't mix paraxial and exact formulas.
- Check sign conventions for Radius of Curvature (usually R>0 is convex from incident side).
